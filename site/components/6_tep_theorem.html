4. The Mount Wilson Equivalence Theorem

The Temporal Equivalence Principle (TEP) reopens the suppressed temporal degree of freedom by restoring an independent dynamical scalar proper-time field. TEP defines two related geometries: the bare gravitational metric ($g_{\mu\nu}$), and the causal matter metric ($\tilde g_{\mu\nu}$) to which standard rods, clocks, and photon fields couple.

These geometries are connected by a universal, dynamical scalar proper-time field ($\phi$). In the canonical preferred-foliation decomposition of Paper 0 (v0.18, §2.2, Step 80) along the unit cosmic temporal flow $U_\mu \equiv -\nabla_\mu\bar\phi/\sqrt{2\bar X}$ ($g^{\mu\nu}U_\mu U_\nu = -1$) and orthogonal spatial projector $\Pi_{\mu\nu} = g_{\mu\nu} + U_\mu U_\nu$, the causal matter metric is defined as:

\begin{equation} \begin{aligned} \tilde g_{\mu\nu} &= -A_{\rm loc}^2(u_{\rm grav}(\mathbf x))\,U_\mu U_\nu + A_{\rm sp}^2(u_{\rm drift}(t,\mathbf x))\,\Pi_{\mu\nu} \\ &\quad + B(\phi)\bigl(\Pi_\mu^\alpha\Pi_\nu^\beta\nabla_\alpha\phi\nabla_\beta\phi - 2(U\cdot\nabla\phi)U_{(\mu}\Pi_{\nu)}{}^\alpha\nabla_\alpha\phi\bigr) , \end{aligned} \end{equation}

written in isotropic conformal–disformal shorthand as $\tilde g_{\mu\nu} = A^2(\phi)\,g_{\mu\nu} + B(\phi)\,\nabla_\mu\phi\,\nabla_\nu\phi$. Here $A_{\rm loc}(u_{\rm grav})$ governs the stationary baryon-sourced temporal lapse $N_{\rm TEP}(\mathbf x) = A_{\rm loc}(u_{\rm grav}(\mathbf x))N_{\rm GR}(\mathbf x)$, $A_{\rm sp}(u_{\rm drift}) = \exp(\beta_A u_{\rm drift})$ governs the secular matter-frame spatial ruler calibration, and $B(\phi)$ governs spatially projected disformal kinetic corrections. On the static, homogeneous cosmological branch ($u_{\rm grav} = 0$, $A_{\rm loc} = 1$, $\Pi_\mu^\alpha\nabla_\alpha\phi = 0$), the spatial gravitational metric is invariant (the scale factor $a = 1$), the spatially projected disformal term vanishes identically, and the cosmological factor is $A_{\rm sp}(t) \equiv A(\phi(t))$.

The following derivation establishes the central result on both the canonical preferred-foliation metric (where $N(t) = A_{\rm loc} = 1$ on the homogeneous background) and the general unprojected isotropic-disformal comparison branch $\tilde g_{\mu\nu} = A^2(\phi)g_{\mu\nu} + B(\phi)\nabla_\mu\phi\nabla_\nu\phi$. On the homogeneous cosmological branch, $\phi = \phi(t)$; define the general effective lapse ($N(t) \equiv 1$ on the preferred-foliation branch, or

\begin{equation} N^2(t) = A^2(\phi) - B(\phi)\,\dot\phi^2 > 0 \end{equation}

on the unprojected isotropic-disformal branch) and denote the cosmological ruler factor by $A(t) \equiv A_{\rm sp}(t)$. The matter-frame line element and comoving proper time are then

\begin{equation} d\tilde s^2 = -N^2\,dt^2 + A^2\,\delta_{ij}\,dx^i dx^j, \qquad d\tilde\tau = N\,dt , \end{equation}

and the normalized comoving matter-frame four-velocity is

\begin{equation} \tilde u^\mu = \left(\frac{1}{N},\, 0,\, 0,\, 0\right) . \end{equation}

Spatial homogeneity supplies three translational Killing vectors. Consequently, the covariant spatial wavevector

\begin{equation} q_i = \tilde g_{ij}\,k^j = A^2\,\delta_{ij}\,k^j \end{equation}

is conserved along the photon trajectory. Writing $q^2 = \delta^{ij}q_i q_j$, the null condition $\tilde g_{\mu\nu}\,k^\mu k^\nu = 0$ gives

\begin{equation} N^2\,(k^0)^2 = \frac{q^2}{A^2} . \end{equation}

The photon frequency measured by a comoving matter observer is

\begin{equation} \omega = -\tilde g_{\mu\nu}\,\tilde u^\mu\,k^\nu = N\,k^0 = \frac{q}{A} . \end{equation}

Since $q$ is conserved,

\begin{equation} \boxed{1 + z = \frac{\omega_{\rm em}}{\omega_0} = \frac{A_{\rm sp}(t_0)}{A_{\rm sp}(t_{\rm em})} \equiv \frac{A_0}{A_{\rm em}}} \end{equation}

On the canonical preferred-foliation metric the spatially projected disformal term vanishes identically on the homogeneous background ($\Pi_\mu^\alpha\nabla_\alpha\phi = 0$), and even on the unprojected isotropic-disformal branch ($A^2 - B\dot\phi^2 > 0$) any homogeneous disformal lapse $N(t)$ cancels identically from the redshift ratio. In inhomogeneous regions, however, the disformal connection can become non-integrable and produce synchronization holonomy.

The Mount Wilson Equivalence Theorem

Mount Wilson Equivalence Theorem. On a homogeneous, static gravitational background with universal matter coupling to the canonical preferred-foliation metric (where $\Pi_\mu^\alpha\nabla_\alpha\phi = 0$, and as a corollary on the unprojected isotropic-disformal branch whenever $A^2 - B\dot\phi^2 > 0$), the endpoint redshift is $1+z = A_{\rm sp}(t_0)/A_{\rm sp}(t_{\rm em}) \equiv A_0/A_{\rm em}$, independently of any homogeneous lapse factor. It is therefore observationally degenerate, at the level of redshift alone, with the FLRW relation $1+z = a_0/a_{\rm em}$.

\begin{equation} 1+z = \frac{\omega_{\rm em}}{\omega_0} = \begin{cases} \dfrac{a_0}{a_{\rm em}}, & \text{FLRW spatial interpretation}, \\[6pt] \dfrac{A_0}{A_{\rm em}}, & \text{TEP temporal interpretation}. \end{cases} \end{equation}

Mount Wilson measured the frequency ratio on the left. The choice between the two geometric descriptions on the right was made by theory. Isochrony selected the spatial branch; TEP reopens the temporal branch. The redshift degeneracy is broken only by observables that compare the gravitational and matter sectors, or by local non-integrable clock transport. Other cosmological observables provide additional consistency tests once a specific temporal-field dynamics is fixed.

The qualification "at the level of redshift alone" is essential. The theorem establishes that the redshift coordinate underlying the original Mount Wilson relation does not, by itself, distinguish an expanding spatial scale factor from dynamical matter-clock transport. The measured spectral shifts can be encoded either as the history of $a(t)$ or as the transport law of $A(\phi)$. Luminosity distance, time dilation, baryon acoustic oscillations, the cosmic microwave background, and standard sirens each require separate consistency checks that are addressed in the specialized TEP corpus.

4.1 Static Space versus Conformal-Frame Equivalence

The equality of the two redshift laws does not make TEP a change of notation. In FLRW, one evolving metric governs matter, light and tensor propagation. In TEP, the gravitational metric remains spatially static while matter and electromagnetic fields couple to the scalar-dependent metric $\tilde g_{\mu\nu}$. A conformal or disformal change of variables may move $A(\phi)$ between sectors, but it cannot simultaneously map both the gravitational propagation sector ($g_{\mu\nu}$) and the causal matter sector ($\tilde g_{\mu\nu}$) to a minimally coupled single-metric FLRW spacetime. The two sectors carry independent physical content; no single field redefinition collapses them into one.

The physical distinction therefore appears in comparisons between the two sectors. Gravitational radiation propagates through $g_{\mu\nu}$, while electromagnetic signals and material detectors are governed by $\tilde g_{\mu\nu}$. Standard-siren gravitational-wave observations paired with electromagnetic counterparts serve as a principal inter-sector discriminator: the gravitational wave measures distance through the bare gravitational frame, while the electromagnetic counterpart measures redshift through the matter frame. Define the dimensionless siren deviation

\begin{equation} \Delta_{\rm siren}(z) \equiv \frac{d_L^{\rm GW}(z)}{d_L^{\rm EM}(z)} - 1 . \end{equation}

Here, $d_L^{\rm GW}$ is the luminosity distance measured from gravitational waves, while $d_L^{\rm EM}$ is the corresponding luminosity distance inferred through electromagnetic observations. If matter and gravity follow different effective geometries, the two need not agree. In minimally coupled single-metric FLRW, both sectors share one metric, so $\Delta_{\rm siren} = 0$. Under the canonical TEP radiation rule (Paper 22), $\Xi(z)=1/(1+z)$ and $\Delta_{\rm siren}=1/(1+z)-1$. In Paper 22 (v0.3, Maputo), the full GWTC-3 bright, dark, and spectral-siren population likelihoods are evaluated under this canonical rule, showing that the apparent $H_0 \simeq 56\ {\rm km\,s^{-1}\,Mpc^{-1}}$ free-fit dark-siren posterior shift ($\Delta\chi^2 = +1.16$) is mechanically driven by $\Lambda$CDM-gated galaxy-catalog host redshifts ($70.0\langle(1+z_{\rm cat})^{-1}\rangle = 55.1\ {\rm km\,s^{-1}\,Mpc^{-1}}$), whereas self-consistent TEP-kernel reassociation at the fixed canonical $H_0 = 68.18\ {\rm km\,s^{-1}\,Mpc^{-1}}$ (Paper 18) recovers the baseline association likelihood ($-2\ln L = 944.31$) without shifting $H_0$, while the separate lab-fixed small-excursion comparison yields $H_0 \simeq 72.2$ versus $72.0\ {\rm km\,s^{-1}\,Mpc^{-1}}$ ($\Delta\chi^2 = +0.20$) at marginal significance. This dimensionless ratio is an invariant inter-sector observable: it cannot be removed by any conformal or disformal field redefinition, because no single transformation simultaneously maps both sectors to a common minimally coupled metric.
